This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. For information about how Google and its advertising partners handle information, please refer to:

Ad personalization and delivery may be restricted depending on your choices and other applicable conditions. Declining consent does not necessarily stop all advertising or all data processing.

Purchases are processed through the platform's official store (e.g., App Store or Google Play). The app does not access or store payment information.

This app contains pre-generated content created or modified with the assistance of generative AI. The app does not provide a feature that sends user input or personal information to a generative AI service, and its AI-assisted content is not generated based on user data.
